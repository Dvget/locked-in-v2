# Product Definition

## Product statement

LOCKED IN is a private fitness app that reliably records strength training, running, weight and activity data, makes complex training data easy to understand, and supports consistent progress with subtle evidence-informed tools.

## Primary purpose

The app should answer:
- What did I do?
- Am I progressing?
- What did I do last time?
- What should I compare this performance against?
- How is my current week going?
- How does this fit into my longer-term development?

## Product boundaries

LOCKED IN is not:
- a social network;
- a nutrition tracker;
- a cloud-account service;
- a fully automatic coach;
- an app that overwhelms the user with goals, badges or alerts.

## Main areas

### Dashboard
Short-term overview and launch point.

Shows the current state of:
- workouts;
- running;
- steps;
- weight.

Each area may include a compact four-week trend line. The Dashboard should not become a dense analytics screen.

### Training
Supports plan-driven strength training with immediate comparison to the last comparable exercise performance.

### Running
Reliable run recording with minimal interaction during the run and useful post-run analysis.

### Progress
Long-term development over the full available history.

### Weekly Report
A compact summary of the past week, comparison with the previous week and contextualization within the longer trend.

### Settings
Configuration and data management, split into:
- Training Plans
- Data & Backup
- Goals
- About LOCKED IN

## Core behavioral objective

The app should make consistent training easier by reducing friction and clearly showing progress.

Consistency matters more than adding more features.
