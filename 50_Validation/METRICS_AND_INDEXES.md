# Metrics and Indexes

## Status

Open for rigorous V2 definition.

## Strength/workout index

Needs a precise definition that:
- works across repeated comparable exercises;
- handles progression in reps and load;
- does not become invalid when alternative exercises are used;
- does not compare fundamentally different exercises as if they were identical;
- remains understandable to the user.

## Running index

Needs a precise definition that can summarize performance/trend without hiding the underlying pace/distance data.

## Alternative exercise rule

Squat and leg press, for example, must keep separate exercise histories.

A workout-level score may aggregate them, but progression for an individual exercise must use that exercise's own history.

## Muscle-group analysis

Future muscle-group analysis should aggregate exercise-level evidence.

Do not claim direct physiological outcomes such as muscle growth from performance metrics alone.

Preferred interpretation:
- exercise performance associated with a muscle group is improving;
- stable;
- declining/stagnating.

## Achievement logic

Potential examples:

Running:
- fastest 1 km;
- fastest 5 km;
- longest run;
- other meaningful personal bests.

Strength:
- exercise PR;
- best volume for an exercise;
- best relevant workout index;
- other genuinely exceptional performance.

Avoid awarding something after every session.
