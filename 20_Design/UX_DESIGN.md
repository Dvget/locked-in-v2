# UX and Design Principles

## Current direction

- Dark-mode-first
- Clean
- Technical
- Modern
- Sleek
- Visually appealing without becoming decorative

The exact visual system is not yet fixed.

## Interaction philosophy

LOCKED IN has two UX layers.

### Fast-use layer
Dashboard, Training and Running should:
- require very few taps;
- surface the important state immediately;
- keep controls intuitive;
- avoid unnecessary menus.

### Deep layer
Progress, plan editing and settings may:
- show more metrics;
- support more controls;
- use deeper navigation when necessary.

## Typography

- clean and technical;
- screen titles should not be excessively large;
- do not repeat obvious context with oversized headings;
- hierarchy must be consistent across screens.

## KPIs

Important metrics such as pace, weight and indexes must be easy to identify, but not dominate every screen as huge hero numbers.

Chart labels and numeric comparisons should be refined to reduce clutter.

## Icons

Use simple, system-like icons consistently.

## Animation

Animations are desirable when they:
- clarify transitions;
- make chart changes feel natural;
- provide useful feedback.

Avoid decorative animation that slows down use.

## Cards

Cards are currently considered a valid structure, especially on Dashboard.

The problem to solve is visual quality, not necessarily the card concept itself.

Preferred direction based on the Strava reference:
- black/dark background;
- slightly lighter charcoal cards;
- large but controlled corner radius;
- clear internal spacing;
- little or no heavy shadow;
- depth mostly through tone separation.

Final card language remains open for design comparison.

## Charts

Desired chart style:
- minimal and calm;
- strong readable line;
- clear data points;
- subtle area fill/gradient/glow;
- restrained grid/axis labels;
- smooth functional transitions.

Dashboard should only use compact trend lines, not full analytical charts.

## Color

Current working hypothesis:
- LOCKED IN brand: orange;
- Running: purple;
- Training: third accent color still open;
- Steps and Weight may remain neutral.

Important:
- avoid five competing section colors;
- use color mainly as accent rather than large filled surfaces;
- final palette must be tested visually on real screens.

## Design decision method

Do not choose the final visual system abstractly.

Create 3–4 variants of the same real LOCKED IN screens and compare:
- color system;
- card styling;
- spacing;
- chart styling;
- typography hierarchy.

Use the same Dashboard and Progress content across variants so the comparison is meaningful.
